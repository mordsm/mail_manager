# Contradiction Analysis: SCFA Butyrate Mucosal Integrity vs High-Dose Toxicity

## Claim A
- **Statement**: Butyrate upregulates claudin-1 and zonula occludens-1, tightening gut barrier and lowering systemic inflammation in clinical cohorts.
- **Source**: `20_Research/could_gut_inflammation_connect_autoimmune_disease_/overview.md`
- **Confidence**: high

## Claim B
- **Statement**: High-concentration local butyrate (>50 mM) induces epithelial stem cell apoptosis and impairs crypt regeneration in mucosal lesions.
- **Source**: `preclinical_in_vitro_crypt_perfusion_study_2023`
- **Confidence**: medium

## Methodological Differences
In-vivo physiological luminal levels (5-15 mM) versus supra-physiological in-vitro crypt immersion models (>50 mM).

## Possible Explanation
Dose-dependent hormetic response curve; physiological levels fuel colonocytes whereas toxic bolus halts mitotic division.

## Current Synthesis
Physiological concentrations provide significant barrier protection; high unbuffered doses risk mucosal crypt blunting.

## Remaining Uncertainty
Therapeutic window and delayed-release formulation pharmacokinetics in active ulcerative flare.
