## Research Question

what are the sources tested and confirmed for autoimmune diseases and specufucky sjogren

## Simple Answer

- Simple answer: autoimmune diseases usually happen when the immune system attacks the body by mistake.
- In the sources found here, the strongest leads are: genetics, immune system confusion, glands and dry-eye/dry-mouth damage, infection or environmental triggers.
- For Sjogren's, the basic idea is: the immune system seems to damage moisture-making glands, which can lead to dry eyes, dry mouth, fatigue, and sometimes other organ problems.
- The PubMed papers are the useful part for causes, because they discuss biology, risk factors, and possible mechanisms.
- The clinical-trial records are less useful for causes; they mostly show what researchers are testing.
- Bottom line: the answer is not one single cause. It looks more like a mix of genes, immune-system misfiring, inflammation, and possible outside triggers.
